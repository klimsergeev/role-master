# Research assignment in the pipeline

Prepare the assignment for the researchers by your own practice. The pipeline adds four requirements.

## Write the questions in English

The researcher writes the report in English and uses your questions, copied verbatim, as its section headings. Write every question and every bound in English, whatever the language of the request and of the skill.

## Split the topic into subjects

- A subject is something with its own sources: a vendor, a product, a method, a body of knowledge.
- Each subject goes to one researcher, and the researchers work in parallel, each in a separate report file.
- Do not split one subject between two researchers, and do not merge two subjects into one assignment.
- A subject about Claude or Claude Code goes to a different researcher than the rest. Keep such questions in a subject of their own.

## Cover every side in the first round

- After the map question, ask about every side of the subject that the «Охват» section of the skill plan needs. A side is a part of the subject that will become content of the skill: a capability, a set of settings, a procedure, a set of ready-made texts, a list of limits.
- On an extension or a rebuild, read the current skill in full first, and ask only about what it does not already hold.
- The second round is the last one. Ask in the first round everything you can already name, and leave to the second only what the first report reveals.

## Return to the script

For each subject return:

- the subject's name;
- the questions, in order. The researcher is told nothing about the skill, so neither the questions nor the bounds mention it or what the material is for;
- in the first round, the path of a ready report when one already covers the subject. Such a subject gets no questions and is not researched again;
- in the second round, the bounds: what exactly to find, where the search stops, what not to bring;
- in the second round, the path of the first-round report on the same subject;
- the allowed sources, copied verbatim from «Охват»;
- whether the subject is about Claude or Claude Code;
- the report path: `Updates/Drafts/[topic folder]/research-[YYYY-MM-DD]-[subject].md`, with `-followup` before `.md` in the second round. The subject in the file name is Latin lowercase with hyphens.

In the second round:

- Write the questions and bounds into the «Недостающее» section of the skill plan. When nothing is missing, write «Нет» there.
- Return whether anything is missing at all. When nothing is missing, return no subjects.
